THE RULES OF THE ARENA

Make the last hit yours.

Two players fight over one 10,000 HP monster each round. Play seven rounds; the highest score wins. Equal scores are a draw.

01 / Shape the fight

After a 3-second countdown, the arena deals 200 damage every second. Toggle auto attack with A: 100 damage every 500 ms. It starts off. Toggling never resets the attack clock.

02 / Commit your Smite

S deals 600 damage after 200 ms. E deals 850 for 2 energy, also after 200 ms. They share one use per round. A submitted cast cannot be canceled, even if it is too early.

03 / Set up a combination

B deals 250 damage after a visible 400 ms windup, costs 1 energy, and can be used once per round. Overlap it with Smite: Burst first, then Smite 200 ms later.

04 / Spend with intent

Start with 6 energy for the entire match. It never regenerates. The server reserves spending immediately; rivals see the cost only at impact. Both Smite windups look identical. Unspent energy has no value after round seven.

05 / Share a lethal tick

All damage due in the same 50 ms server tick lands as a batch. If both players contribute to a lethal batch—even with auto attacks—both earn a point. A player plus arena damage awards that player a point. Arena damage alone awards none.

06 / Read the other player

Stop attacking to change the pace. Watch revealed spending and previous casts. Waiting risks losing the objective; spending now limits later rounds. Energy costs for casts that finish after a kill still reveal at their scheduled impact; they deal no further damage.

Desktop: A / S / E / B. Mobile: tap the action cards. Action timing uses the server clock, never your device timestamp. Unequal connections are not perfectly fair. A disconnect pauses play for up to 30 seconds before the remaining player may claim a forfeit. Refresh this tab to reconnect. Rooms expire after two hours; server restarts clear rooms.
