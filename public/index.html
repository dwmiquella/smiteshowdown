THE RULES OF THE ARENA

Make the last hit yours.

Two players fight over one 10,000 HP monster each round. Play seven rounds; the highest score wins. Equal scores are a draw.

01 / Shape the fight

Each monster has 10,000 HP. After a 3-second countdown, the arena deals 200 damage every second. A toggles 100-damage attacks every 500 ms. They start off; toggling never resets their clock.

02 / Your Smite evolves

S casts free Smite after 200 ms, once per round. Damage rises by 100 each round: 600, 700, 800, 900, 1,000, 1,100, 1,200. Its health-bar marker follows the current damage. Committed casts cannot be canceled.

03 / Burst or disrupt

B: 250 damage after 400 ms, 1 energy. D: Disrupt stuns the rival for 600 ms after a visible 300 ms windup, 1 energy. Each is available once per round. Abilities may overlap.

04 / Recover each round

Start with 3 energy. Recover 1 at the start of rounds 2–7, up to a cap of 6. Ready-up cannot farm energy. The server reserves costs immediately; rivals see spending at impact. A rematch resets energy to 3.

05 / First lethal hit wins

Damage resolves one hit at a time. Equal-time spells resolve in the order the server accepted them. If your first Smite kills, only you score; if it leaves HP, the later hit can win. Casts precede arena damage, then auto attacks. Auto-attack tie priority alternates each round and is displayed. Arena kills give no points.

06 / Beat the stun

While stunned, you cannot submit new actions and your auto attacks skip their scheduled hits. Already committed casts still resolve—even Disrupt. Two committed Disrupts can stun both players. Attacks resume on their original cadence. Pausing also freezes stun duration. A kill clears stuns; later casts spend their reserved cost but do nothing.

Desktop: A / S / D / B. Mobile: tap the action cards. Action timing uses the server clock, never your device timestamp. Unequal connections are not perfectly fair. A disconnect pauses play for up to 30 seconds before the remaining player may claim a forfeit. Refresh this tab to reconnect. Rooms expire after two hours; server restarts clear rooms.
